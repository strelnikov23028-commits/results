Don't confuse the microscope body with the sealed box. The kit contains the plastic body of the microscope itself. The sealed incubation box (37 °C + CO₂) built around the whole microscope is a separate laser‑cut enclosure (Block C).
Check the kit's plastic for heat resistance. The microscope lives permanently at 37 °C in a humid box. Confirm the body material: if it's PLA it will warp at 37 °C and must be reprinted in ASA or PETG. ASA/PETG is fine.
STAGE 1 — start immediately

The robot works

Brightfield (already in the kit), cheap cells. Verify: holds 37 °C and CO₂, autofocus stable, catches division, controlled online. Independent of a biology lab.

STAGE 2 — order optics now, use later

See the centrioles

Fluorescence + labelled cells. Requires a confirmed lab. Objective, filters and excitation LEDs have the longest lead time — order them first.

Three rules you must not break

  • 1
    Plastic living at 37 °C — only ASA/PETG, never PLA. Applies to the kit body (verify the material) and any extra printing.
  • 2
    CO₂ sensor — 0–20% range only. Sensirion SCD30 won't work: it reads up to ~4%, while the setpoint is 5%.
  • 3
    Labelled cells (stage 2) — do not order until a lab is confirmed. Stage‑1 cells can and should be ordered (Block D).

BLOCK B · order first (long lead time)

Optics & fluorescence

The kit only has the condenser and brightfield light. Everything for magnification and fluorescence is added here. The specs below are mandatory, not preferences.

6

Objective 40×, dry, RMS thread

RMSNA 0.75finite / 160 mmparfocal 45 mmnot ∞ · no Ph · not NA 0.95

Critical: RMS thread, 160 mm finite (marking 160/0.17, not «∞»), parfocal 45 mm, dry, no phase ring (Ph). Take NA 0.75 for ~⅓ more light. A used Nikon/Zeiss/Olympus fluorite fits best; NA 0.65 achromats also work.

Engraved 40, NA and 160/0.17 (if ∞ — return). Lenses free of fungus, scratches, haze.
$25–450
